The BuilderDogs Legends Report · Issue #5

Week of Nov 16 – 22, 2026 · from BuilderDogs (@BooMetaX)

Published Nov 23, 2026

New to Doginal Dogs Legends?

Doginal Dogs Legends (DDL) is a trading card game played with physical cards on a table. The first set, Rise of the Pack, has 111 hand-drawn cards. Two players each bring a 40-card deck and try to take the other player's Hero from 40 HP down to 0 (ddltcg.com/rules). The art comes from Doginal Dogs, a collection of 10,000 pixel-art dogs on the Dogecoin blockchain, but the card game itself needs no crypto. You can try it free in your browser at ddltcg.com/play.

Top story: the first DDL Championship is underway

Last week, buying the $99 Victorious Gary promo card was the way to qualify. This week, players signed up and the games began.

  • Sign-up (Nov 18): @DDLTCG opened a 48-hour registration window for anyone who bought a Victorious Gary promo card. Players had to fill out a form and join the Doginal Dogs Discord, and @DDLTCG said it would post a schedule once registration closed. Source (Nov 18)
  • Kickoff (posted Nov 22): "The first DDLTCG Championship starts TOMORROW!" Players check in on Discord at 10am ET (9am Central) on Monday, Nov 23 to see who they play that day. Fans can watch every tournament game at ddltcg.com/play. Source (Nov 22)
  • Format: "Swiss-style matchups" run all week, and "the top scorers will face off in knockout rounds on Saturday night" (that's Nov 28). In a Swiss format, nobody is knocked out early: everyone plays a set number of rounds, each time against someone with a similar record, and the best records move on. Source (Nov 22)
  • The finish line: per the official Oct 24 announcement, the top 16 go to the in-person final on Dec 9, 11am to 3pm, at Collectors Club in Miami, during DD Miami (Dec 7 to 9) (details, Oct 24). The Nov 22 post didn't repeat the top-16 number.

Players were fired up. "BABE WAKE UP, ITS TIME TO REGISTER," posted @MetalXMeta (Nov 18). @homeMetaX called it "the last challenge to prove your worth" (Nov 18), and @beatdropbri announced plans to be "representing Unknown Collar" at the tournament in Miami (Nov 19).

New promo card: Santa Gary, free with Black Friday orders

On Nov 20, @doginaldogs kicked off its official Black Friday sale on the official shop: "everything we have is up for grabs one last time," limited stock, while supplies last (post, Nov 20). The card news came in the follow-up: a free Santa Gary promo card is added automatically to orders of $299 or more. It's not sold separately, and it's only while supplies last (post with card image, Nov 20). The shop page (checked Nov 23) says the same thing.

The card image shows Gary in a Santa hat and beard, riding a sleigh full of presents. The label reads promo V116, a Neutral Creature, and the card text reads "Play: Draw a card." (Image by @doginaldogs, so we link to it rather than show it here.) @Andmetax says he already has his: "I GOT MY SANTA GARY FOR FREE" (Nov 20).

Collector sleuthing (just a guess for now): @Astrozohmbie noticed that Victorious Gary is numbered V112 and Santa Gary is V116. The guess: three more promo cards, numbered in between, that we haven't seen yet (Nov 20). @barkmeta replied, "this guy is paying attention" (Nov 20). Nothing official has announced more promos, so treat it as a fun theory.

Collectors' corner

  • Fresh slabs. @PeteSake813 got a batch of cards back from AGS grading: "I absolutely love these slabs... That Atlas though!" (Nov 20). @DDLTCG reposted it Nov 21. A slab is a card sealed in a hard plastic case with a grade on the label.
  • A finished master set. @Andmetax says he completed his Rise of the Pack master set (one of every card in the set) back at DDNYC: "THE PACK IS JUST GETTING STARTED" (Nov 20). He followed up with a photo of a "Legends night": "BOXES ON THE TABLE AND SLABS ON DISPLAY" (Nov 21).
  • Binder tour. @homeMetaX kept sharing pages from his master-set binder: the Pirate squad (Nov 16, which @DDLTCG reposted), page 24 with the Shiboshi card (Nov 17), page 25 with Barron and Penny (Nov 18), and page 26, the last Pirates page (Nov 22).
  • Cutest card award. @eagerbtc's pick for the cutest card is Sweetie (Nov 16). @DDLTCG reposted it.
  • Card spotlight: Penny. @BoxMetaAlt describes Penny as an Epic full-art Pirate that costs 6 mana, is a "6/2" (6 attack, 2 health), and brings back a Creature costing 4 or less from your graveyard (your discard pile) when you play it (Nov 22). @barkmeta's reply: "love penny." That's a fan's summary of the card, not the official card text.

Learn the game

@emeta777's Blog #43 covers traps, cards you play face-down that go off later. It shows how setting, holding and resolving traps keeps close games clear. It's a community guide, not an official rules document. Blog post · Post (Nov 19)

Want to see how good players handle traps? Championship games are open to watch this week at ddltcg.com/play.
