New to Doginal Dogs Legends?

Doginal Dogs Legends (DDL) is a trading card game played with physical cards on a table. The first set, Rise of the Pack, has 111 cards. Two players each bring a 40-card deck and try to take the other player's Hero from 40 HP down to 0 (ddltcg.com/rules). The art comes from Doginal Dogs, a collection of 10,000 pixel-art dogs on the Dogecoin blockchain, but the card game itself needs no crypto. You can try it free in your browser at ddltcg.com/play.

Top story: DD Miami is 30 days out

On Nov 7, @doginaldogs posted that DDMIAMI 2026 is 30 days away. Source (Nov 7)

The event runs Dec 7 to 9, and its page lists the DDL Miami Championship and Dog Pod at the Arlo Wynwood (doginaldogs.com/events-2026/ddmiami-2026). Graded Legends cards are here

Collectors Club in Wynwood, Miami partners with AGS for same-day grading at $30 per card flat, with slabs back in about an hour, subject to lab availability (Collectors Club). A slab is a card sealed in a protective case with its grade on the label.

Separate: The Do Only Good Everyday Foundation

The Do Only Good Everyday Foundation describes itself as a federally recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofit serving children (FAQ). Its first event, Big Hearts for Little Smiles, took place Sept 3 during DDNYC week at the New York Center for Child Development in Spanish Harlem, where community-raised funds put Gary & Mary dog plushies in the hands of children and their families (source). Community posts described it as the foundation's first activation for kids with autism (source). On Nov 5 the foundation posted that it is getting ready for December with the South Florida Autism Charter School (source). It is a separate organization, not part of BuilderDogs, and this is not a donation ask.
