New to Doginal Dogs Legends?

Doginal Dogs Legends (DDL) is a trading card game played with physical cards on a table. The first set, Rise of the Pack, has 111 cards. Two players each bring a 40-card deck and try to take the other player's Hero from 40 HP down to 0 (ddltcg.com/rules). The art comes from Doginal Dogs, a collection of 10,000 pixel-art dogs on the Dogecoin blockchain, but the card game itself needs no crypto. You can try it free in your browser at ddltcg.com/play.

Top story: Championship registration opened, and Victorious Gary was the ticket

On Nov 10, @DDLTCG opened registration for the first official DDL Championship. Source (Nov 10)

  • How you got in: the registration link went to the Victorious Gary Promo Card on the official shop, $99. The shop page says the card is the only way to enter, entries are limited to one per buyer no matter how many cards you buy, the entry has no cash value and can't be transferred, and the card ships in December 2026 (shop page, checked Nov 17). The listing showed Sold out when checked Nov 17.
  • Deadline: registration was open until Monday, Nov 16 at 12AM ET, which is 11 PM Sunday, Nov 15 in Central time. Source (Nov 10). It is now closed.
  • What players get: every participant receives one exclusive Giga Rare Gary promo card. Source (Nov 10)
  • The event itself: per the official Oct 24 announcement, the Championship is Dec 9, 11am to 3pm, at Collectors Club in Miami (source, Oct 24). Registered players play an online round robin in the week before, and the top 16 advance to the in-person final (details, Oct 24). It's part of DD Miami, Dec 7 to 9 (event page). The event page notes the schedule is subject to change.

The timeline filled up with "Victorious Gary secured" posts after registration opened, from @PawsMeta (Nov 12) and @HawaiiSol (Nov 11) to @corey_on_x, who says he'll grade all five of his at DD Miami (Nov 10).

New card reveal: Victorious Gary

The art shows Gary in gold armor on a throne. The card is labeled promo V112, a Neutral Creature, and its text reads "Play: Draw a card." That text is from the official @DDLTCG card image above; the promo is not in the 111-card Rise of the Pack gallery on ddltcg.com. A promo card is a special card you can't get in booster packs (shop page).

Graded cards: a first GEM-MT 10 Bark

On Nov 9, @Andmetax posted what he calls the first @DDLTCG Bark card graded GEM-MT 10 by AGS. He says five Bark cards have been graded so far and this is the only 10. "GEM-MT 10" is the top grade on the label of a slab, a card sealed in a protective case. He listed it on eBay at $1,669.69 or best offer. That is an asking price, not a sale. Source (Nov 9)
