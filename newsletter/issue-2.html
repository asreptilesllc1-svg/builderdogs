New to Doginal Dogs Legends?

Doginal Dogs Legends (DDL) is a physical trading card game. Its first set, Rise of the Pack, has 111 hand-drawn cards across five classes of Creatures, Spells and Traps (ddltcg.com). Two players each bring a 40-card deck, with up to 3 copies of any card, and try to take the other player's Hero from 40 HP to 0 (ddltcg.com/rules).

The art comes from Doginal Dogs, a collection of 10,000 pixel-art dogs recorded as digital collectibles on the Dogecoin blockchain (doginaldogs.com). Dogecoin is a cryptocurrency, and a "Doginal" is a piece of digital art written permanently onto it. You don't need any crypto to play: the game is cards on a table.

How to start:

  • Learn: the official rules at ddltcg.com/rules.
  • Try it: the free browser beta at ddltcg.com/play has a built-in tutorial.
  • Collect: the official booster box (24 packs of 10 cards) is $179 on shop.doginaldogs.com. As of Nov 2, the shop shows it as not available (sold out). Singles trade on eBay, and builderdogs.com tracks what they actually sell for.

  • Bots saying GM: all week, @TwinTowerCity's Grok Bot posted good-morning and good-night messages to the pack. Example (Nov 1)

Stay safe while you build: Connecting a bot to any shared space can give it access to your files and logins. Read every permission screen before you connect. Never give a bot your seed phrase (the master password to a crypto wallet). And never let it sign wallet transactions you don't understand. Boo shared the same advice this week: AI makes scams look real, so check links somewhere else before you click. Source (Oct 26)

Grading corner

Grading means sending a card to a company that checks its condition and seals it in a plastic case (a "slab") with a score from 1 to 10. A 10 is "Gem Mint."

  • @Andmetax launched Pop Report Galaxy, a tracker of every AGS-graded Legends slab he could find: 47 so far. His question for the pack: which Rise of the Pack card is hardest to get a 10 on? Launch (Oct 28) · Follow-up (Oct 29)
  • He also said his AGS slab sold on eBay for over $200. Source (Oct 31). See the Market snapshot below.

Data note: builderdogs.com labels its card prices "eBay sold, through Oct 23, 2026," and its single-card (ungraded) sales also stop on Oct 23. No ungraded single-card sales from this week are in the data yet. The site did log two newer sales:

  • 15 sealed Rise of the Pack booster packs: sold for $150 on an accepted offer (Nov 1), about $10 a pack. For comparison, the official $179 box works out to about $7.46 a pack. builderdogs.com/sets-sales

These are past sales, not price predictions. Most cards have only a few sales, so treat any single sale as a data point, not a value.
