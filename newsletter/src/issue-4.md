## New to Doginal Dogs Legends?
Doginal Dogs Legends (DDL) is a trading card game played with physical cards on a table. The first set, Rise of the Pack, has 111 cards. Two players each bring a 40-card deck and try to take the other player's Hero from 40 HP down to 0 ([ddltcg.com/rules](https://ddltcg.com/rules)). The art comes from Doginal Dogs, a collection of 10,000 pixel-art dogs on the Dogecoin blockchain, but the card game itself needs no crypto. You can try it free in your browser at [ddltcg.com/play](https://ddltcg.com/play).

## Top story: Championship registration opened, and Victorious Gary was the ticket
![DDL Legends TCG Tournament Pass, Miami, Rise of the Pack](img/issue-4/ddltcg-championship-registration-2026-11-10.jpg)
*Image: @DDLTCG. [Original post](https://x.com/DDLTCG/status/2120275305415975226)*

On Nov 10, @DDLTCG opened registration for the first official DDL Championship. [Source](https://x.com/DDLTCG/status/2120275305415975226) (Nov 10)
- **How you got in:** the registration link went to the Victorious Gary Promo Card on the official shop, $99. The shop page says the card is the **only** way to enter, entries are limited to **one per buyer** no matter how many cards you buy, the entry has no cash value and can't be transferred, and the card **ships in December 2026** ([shop page](https://shop.doginaldogs.com/products/victorious-gary-promo-card), checked Nov 17). The listing showed Sold out when checked Nov 17.
- **Deadline:** registration was open until Monday, Nov 16 at 12AM ET, which is 11 PM Sunday, Nov 15 in Central time. [Source](https://x.com/DDLTCG/status/2120275306053616002) (Nov 10). It is now closed.
- **What players get:** every participant receives one exclusive Giga Rare Gary promo card. [Source](https://x.com/DDLTCG/status/2120275306410099079) (Nov 10)
- **The event itself:** per the official Oct 24 announcement, the Championship is **Dec 9, 11am to 3pm, at Collectors Club in Miami** ([source](https://x.com/DDLTCG/status/2114028202255331698), Oct 24). Registered players play an online round robin in the week before, and the top 16 advance to the in-person final ([details](https://x.com/DDLTCG/status/2114028230365553028), Oct 24). It's part of DD Miami, Dec 7 to 9 ([event page](https://doginaldogs.com/events-2026/ddmiami-2026)). The event page notes the schedule is subject to change.

The timeline filled up with "Victorious Gary secured" posts after registration opened, from @PawsMeta ([Nov 12](https://x.com/PawsMeta/status/2120926602066563502)) and @HawaiiSol ([Nov 11](https://x.com/HawaiiSol/status/2120679520865014005)) to @corey_on_x, who says he'll grade all five of his at DD Miami ([Nov 10](https://x.com/corey_on_x/status/2120295771975119116)).

## New card reveal: Victorious Gary
![Victorious Gary promo card, V112, Creature, Neutral, in a display case](img/issue-4/ddltcg-giga-rare-victorious-gary-promo-2026-11-10.jpg)
*Image: @DDLTCG. [Original post](https://x.com/DDLTCG/status/2120275306410099079)*

The art shows Gary in gold armor on a throne. The card is labeled promo **V112**, a Neutral Creature, and its text reads "Play: Draw a card." That text is from the official @DDLTCG card image above; the promo is not in the 111-card Rise of the Pack gallery on ddltcg.com. A promo card is a special card you can't get in booster packs ([shop page](https://shop.doginaldogs.com/products/victorious-gary-promo-card)).

## Graded cards: a first GEM-MT 10 Bark
On Nov 9, @Andmetax posted what he calls the first @DDLTCG Bark card graded GEM-MT 10 by AGS. He says five Bark cards have been graded so far and this is the only 10. "GEM-MT 10" is the top grade on the label of a slab, a card sealed in a protective case. He listed it on eBay at $1,669.69 or best offer. That is an **asking price, not a sale**. [Source](https://x.com/Andmetax/status/2119942155871436805) (Nov 9)
