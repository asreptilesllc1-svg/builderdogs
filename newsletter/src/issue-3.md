## New to Doginal Dogs Legends?
Doginal Dogs Legends (DDL) is a trading card game played with physical cards on a table. The first set, Rise of the Pack, has 111 cards. Two players each bring a 40-card deck and try to take the other player's Hero from 40 HP down to 0 ([ddltcg.com/rules](https://ddltcg.com/rules)). The art comes from Doginal Dogs, a collection of 10,000 pixel-art dogs on the Dogecoin blockchain, but the card game itself needs no crypto. You can try it free in your browser at [ddltcg.com/play](https://ddltcg.com/play).

## Graded Legends cards are here
Collectors Club in Wynwood, Miami partners with AGS for same-day grading at $30 per card flat, with slabs back in about an hour, subject to lab availability ([Collectors Club](https://packs.collectorsclub.com/hub/grading)). A slab is a card sealed in a protective case with its grade on the label.
- On Nov 6, @homeMetaX posted that Miami will have same-day grading on site at Collectors Club. That is a community post, not an official announcement. [Source](https://x.com/homeMetaX/status/2118712513252540755) (Nov 6)
- @Andmetax says he is grading his Legends cards with AGS. [Source](https://x.com/Andmetax/status/2118716396079132773) (Nov 6)

## Separate: The Do Only Good Everyday Foundation
The Do Only Good Everyday Foundation describes itself as a federally recognized 501(c)(3) nonprofit serving children ([FAQ](https://doonlygoodeveryday.org/faq)). Its first event, Big Hearts for Little Smiles, took place Sept 3 during DDNYC week at the New York Center for Child Development in Spanish Harlem, where community-raised funds put Gary & Mary dog plushies in the hands of children and their families ([source](https://x.com/NebMeta/status/2095704909471490452)). Community posts described it as the foundation's first activation for kids with autism ([source](https://x.com/veemeta/status/2088758250078339357)). On Nov 5 the foundation posted that it is getting ready for December with the South Florida Autism Charter School ([source](https://x.com/DoOnlyGood__/status/2118389023454499015)). It is a separate organization, not part of BuilderDogs, and this is not a donation ask.
