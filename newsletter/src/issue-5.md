## New to Doginal Dogs Legends?
Doginal Dogs Legends (DDL) is a trading card game played with physical cards on a table. The first set, Rise of the Pack, has 111 hand-drawn cards. Two players each bring a 40-card deck and try to take the other player's Hero from 40 HP down to 0 ([ddltcg.com/rules](https://ddltcg.com/rules)). The art comes from Doginal Dogs, a collection of 10,000 pixel-art dogs on the Dogecoin blockchain, but the card game itself needs no crypto. You can try it free in your browser at [ddltcg.com/play](https://ddltcg.com/play).

## Top story: the first DDL Championship is underway
![DDL Legends TCG Tournament Pass, Miami, with a "Live now" tag: Tournament is open](img/issue-5/ddltcg-championship-starts-2026-11-22.jpg)
*Image: @DDLTCG. [Original post](https://x.com/DDLTCG/status/2124623975791681784)*

Last week, buying the $99 Victorious Gary promo card was the way to qualify. This week, players signed up and the games began.
- **Sign-up (Nov 18):** @DDLTCG opened a 48-hour registration window for anyone who bought a Victorious Gary promo card. Players had to fill out a form and join the Doginal Dogs Discord, and @DDLTCG said it would post a schedule once registration closed. [Source](https://x.com/DDLTCG/status/2123071230500536492) (Nov 18)
- **Kickoff (posted Nov 22):** "The first DDLTCG Championship starts TOMORROW!" Players check in on Discord at **10am ET (9am Central) on Monday, Nov 23** to see who they play that day. Fans can **watch every tournament game at [ddltcg.com/play](https://ddltcg.com/play)**. [Source](https://x.com/DDLTCG/status/2124623975791681784) (Nov 22)
- **Format:** "Swiss-style matchups" run all week, and "the top scorers will face off in knockout rounds on Saturday night" (that's Nov 28). In a Swiss format, nobody is knocked out early: everyone plays a set number of rounds, each time against someone with a similar record, and the best records move on. [Source](https://x.com/DDLTCG/status/2124623975791681784) (Nov 22)
- **The finish line:** per the official Oct 24 announcement, the top 16 go to the in-person final on **Dec 9, 11am to 3pm, at Collectors Club in Miami**, during DD Miami (Dec 7 to 9) ([details](https://x.com/DDLTCG/status/2114028186531185082), Oct 24). The Nov 22 post didn't repeat the top-16 number.

*Image: @DDLTCG. [Original post](https://x.com/DDLTCG/status/2123071230500536492)*

"BABE WAKE UP, ITS TIME TO REGISTER," posted @MetalXMeta ([Nov 18](https://x.com/MetalXMeta/status/2123082520547999872)). @homeMetaX called it "the last challenge to prove your worth" ([Nov 18](https://x.com/homeMetaX/status/2123072536984375554)), and @beatdropbri announced plans to be "representing Unknown Collar" at the tournament in Miami ([Nov 19](https://x.com/beatdropbri/status/2123504094430052602)).

## New promo card: Santa Gary, free with Black Friday orders
On Nov 20, @doginaldogs kicked off its official Black Friday sale on the [official shop](https://shop.doginaldogs.com/): "everything we have is up for grabs one last time," limited stock, while supplies last ([post](https://x.com/doginaldogs/status/2123827320041418892), Nov 20). The card news came in the follow-up: a **free Santa Gary promo card** is added automatically to orders of **$299 or more**. It's **not sold separately**, and it's only while supplies last ([post with card image](https://x.com/doginaldogs/status/2123830526666555634), Nov 20). The [shop page](https://shop.doginaldogs.com/products/santa-gary-ddl-promo-card) (checked Nov 23) says the same thing.

The card image shows Gary in a Santa hat and beard, riding a sleigh full of presents. The label reads promo **V116**, a Neutral Creature, and the card text reads "Play: Draw a card." (Image by @doginaldogs, so we link to it rather than show it here.) @Andmetax says he already has his: "I GOT MY SANTA GARY FOR FREE" ([Nov 20](https://x.com/Andmetax/status/2123984096678289545)).

**Collector sleuthing (just a guess for now):** @Astrozohmbie noticed that Victorious Gary is numbered V112 and Santa Gary is V116. The guess: three more promo cards, numbered in between, that we haven't seen yet ([Nov 20](https://x.com/Astrozohmbie/status/2123921968588370016)). @barkmeta replied, "this guy is paying attention" ([Nov 20](https://x.com/barkmeta/status/2123932819034628330)). Nothing official has announced more promos, so treat it as a fun theory.
