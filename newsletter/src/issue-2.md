## New to Doginal Dogs Legends?
Doginal Dogs Legends (DDL) is a physical trading card game. Its first set, Rise of the Pack, has 111 hand-drawn cards across five classes of Creatures, Spells and Traps ([ddltcg.com](https://ddltcg.com/)). Two players each bring a 40-card deck, with up to 3 copies of any card, and try to take the other player's Hero from 40 HP to 0 ([ddltcg.com/rules](https://ddltcg.com/rules)).

The art comes from Doginal Dogs, a collection of 10,000 pixel-art dogs recorded as digital collectibles on the Dogecoin blockchain ([doginaldogs.com](https://www.doginaldogs.com/)). Dogecoin is a cryptocurrency, and a "Doginal" is a piece of digital art written permanently onto it. You don't need any crypto to play: the game is cards on a table.

How to start:
- **Learn:** the official rules at [ddltcg.com/rules](https://ddltcg.com/rules).
- **Try it:** the free browser beta at [ddltcg.com/play](https://ddltcg.com/play) has a built-in tutorial.
- **Collect:** the official booster box (24 packs of 10 cards) is $179 on [shop.doginaldogs.com](https://shop.doginaldogs.com/products/doginal-dogs-legends-rise-of-the-pack-booster-box). As of Nov 2, the shop shows it as **not available (sold out)**. Singles trade on eBay, and [builderdogs.com](/legends) tracks what they actually sell for.

- **"The party never stops":** @MilkXMeta invited the pack to a 24/7 Doginal Dogs metaverse with a live stage, voxel dogs in top hats and wizard hats, DJs, yachts and games. [Source](https://x.com/MilkXMeta/status/2115221369835454966) (Oct 27)
- **Bots saying GM:** all week, @TwinTowerCity's Grok Bot posted good-morning and good-night messages to the pack. [Example](https://x.com/TwinTowerCity/status/2116887369442115778) (Nov 1)

**Stay safe while you build:** Connecting a bot to any shared space can give it access to your files and logins. Read every permission screen before you connect. Never give a bot your seed phrase (the master password to a crypto wallet). And never let it sign wallet transactions you don't understand. Boo shared the same advice this week: AI makes scams look real, so check links somewhere else before you click. [Source](https://x.com/BooMetaX/status/2114744760028020969) (Oct 26)

## Grading corner
Grading means sending a card to a company that checks its condition and seals it in a plastic case (a "slab") with a score from 1 to 10. A 10 is "Gem Mint."
- @Andmetax launched **Pop Report Galaxy**, a tracker of every AGS-graded Legends slab he could find: **47 so far**. His question for the pack: which Rise of the Pack card is hardest to get a 10 on? [Launch](https://x.com/Andmetax/status/2115628246221897778) (Oct 28) · [Follow-up](https://x.com/Andmetax/status/2115795286878519317) (Oct 29)
