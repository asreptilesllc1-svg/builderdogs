New to Doginal Dogs Legends?

Doginal Dogs Legends (DDL) is a physical trading card game. Its first set, Rise of the Pack, has 111 hand-drawn cards across five classes of Creatures, Spells and Traps (ddltcg.com). Two players each bring a 40-card deck, with up to 3 copies of any card, and try to bring the other player's Hero from 40 HP to 0 (ddltcg.com/rules).

The art is inspired by Doginal Dogs, a collection of 10,000 pixel-art dogs recorded as digital collectibles on the Dogecoin blockchain (doginaldogs.com). Dogecoin is a cryptocurrency; a "Doginal" is a piece of digital art inscribed, or permanently written, onto it. The card game itself is played with cards on a table.

How to start:

  • Learn: read the official rules at ddltcg.com/rules.
  • Try it: the browser beta at ddltcg.com/play has a built-in tutorial.
  • Collect: a booster box (24 packs of 10 cards) is listed at $179 on shop.doginaldogs.com. Singles trade on eBay; builderdogs.com tracks what they actually sell for.

Top story: the first official DDL Championship

On Oct 24, @DDLTCG announced the first official DDLTCG Championship: Dec 9, 11am–3pm, at Collectors Club in Miami. Everyone who registers plays an online round robin the week before, and the top 16 advance to the in-person final. Prizes: 1st gets an ultra-rare Gary promo card, a rare Doginal Dog and 10 booster boxes; 2nd gets the Gary promo and 5 boxes; 3rd gets 5 boxes. Registration form: see the official thread.
Source: x.com/DDLTCG/status/2114028186531185082 (Oct 24)
